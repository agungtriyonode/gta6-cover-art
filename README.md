Neon Vice Cover Studio: a GTA-style cover art maker that turns your photos and a caption into a multi-panel Vice City poster — three layouts (Mosaic, Strip, Duo), three photo slots with crop and colour-grade controls per panel, procedural demo photos, randomise, undo/redo, and PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Fully offline: no network requests, no libraries, no external fonts — photos stay on your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
